How we estimated this
  1. Staff: 96. From a company database search (ZoomInfo). Medium confidence.
  2. Revenue: $18.4M est. Modelled by ZoomInfo, not reported. Check: 96 staff × $406K revenue per employee at listed electrical contractors = $39.0M ceiling; the estimate is 47% of that rate. Medium confidence.
  3. EBITDA: $1.8M est. Revenue × 10%. Listed electrical peers earn 11.2% EBITDA margins (median); 10% assumed for a private contractor. Low confidence.
  4. Likely price: $11.6M–$12.7M est. EBITDA × 6.3x to 6.9x, the average for deals under $25M of value (GF Data). The model starts at 6.5x: $12.0M. Low confidence.
  5. Financing: $5.5M debt, $6.8M equity est. Debt of 3.0x EBITDA at 7.8%, the current market averages (GF Data), plus 3% deal fees. Market average.
  6. Return: 16.1% a year est. Sold at the same 6.5x after 5 years, before any gain from merging into CET. Low confidence.
