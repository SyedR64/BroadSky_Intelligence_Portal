The rubric

Ten criteria, weighted the way the deals imply

Each criterion scores 1 to 5. The Broad Sky fit score uses the five criteria the add-on screens can test per target, at their rubric weights, on a 0–100 scale.

How to read the score. The add-on screens were built to find companies that fit each company, so most targets clear the 70 gate; the rubric’s job is ordering. Recurring revenue, tech upside and the bench below the owner are 26% of the weight and come from the first call, which is why the script below asks for them.
