Growth plan · October 2026

Two states today. Six-state grid by month 36.

About 3x revenue in 36 months: make MA and CT one company, fill in the other four New England states and shift the mix toward recurring O&M.

Terms: CET: Commonwealth Electrical Technologies. O&M: operations and maintenance. SRF: state revolving fund for water projects. WWTP: wastewater treatment plant. IBEW and NECA: the electricians' union and its contractor association. IRR: internal rate of return.
