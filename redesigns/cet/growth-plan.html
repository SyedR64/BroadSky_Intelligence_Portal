Growth plan · for the Broad Sky Portfolio Resource Group (PRG) · Oct 2026

Two states today. Six-state grid by month 36.

Smith + Howard went from one Atlanta office to 11 locations, nine add-ons and about 4x revenue in 3.7 years. CET starts bigger, with Horton and NuWave already inside, so the plan is about 3x in 36 months: make MA and CT one company, fill in the other four New England states CET is already licensed in, and move the mix toward recurring O&M work. That mix shift is what earns a higher multiple.

    05 · Talent

    The real constraint is licensed electricians, not demand.

    CET’s OSHA records show it as a merit shop. Some targets are union. The plan runs two apprenticeship lanes, claims the MA and CT tax credits, shortens the time to a journeyman license and gives foremen equity.

    06 · The add-on machine

    48 screened targets. Call these first.

    Ranked from the portal’s add-on screen (fit out of 100: capability, geography, scale, ownership readiness). Revenue figures are ZoomInfo models and still need checking.

    07 · Financing and returns

    How it gets funded, and what it is worth at exit.

    Funding follows the Horton precedent: delayed-draw debt plus seller rollover, with co-invest for the bigger add-on wave. Value comes from organic EBITDA growth, EBITDA bought through add-ons (net of what is paid for it) and a quality premium for the shift to recurring, tech-enabled earnings. Every figure is an estimate est.

        A single-line diagram as the heroElectrical engineers read single-line diagrams every day, so the expansion is drawn as feeders and a breaker: energized MA and CT sites and planned RI, NH, ME and VT feeders. The open breaker shows that anything beyond New England is outside the plan. It tells the CET team the plan is written in their language, and shows a lender at a glance that the numbers are New England only.
        Lead with the numberA KPI-first hero and one accent colour on a calm canvas. The four numbers the deal team gets asked about (revenue, EBITDA, people, sites) are on the first screen, so the page works as a 30-second read before an investment committee or lender call.
        Entry to exit on a dated timelineEach CET move is mapped onto the growth plan that already produced a Broad Sky exit (Smith + Howard), which makes the plan easier for the PRG, lenders and a future buyer to believe. KPI moved: time to a committee-ready plan.
        Show the mix, not just the sizeEach capability segment is shown on its own line, and acquired founders keep their brands inside one partner network. The levers and add-on sections show a buyer a growing recurring mix and a repeatable add-on cadence. KPIs moved: recurring share, add-ons closed per year, exit multiple.
