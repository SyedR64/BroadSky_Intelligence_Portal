06Risks

What could go wrong — and the hedge.

A tech thesis in a field-services business fails on adoption, not software. Each risk below has an owner and a mitigation built into the roadmap.

High

Field adoption

Foremen reject another app; data stays in notebooks and the dashboards go stale.

Hedge: mobile-first, one foreman champion per office, daily-log completion tied to job bonuses.

High

OT cybersecurity

Remote access to municipal SCADA widens the attack surface of a water system.

Hedge: segmented networks, MFA, no inbound ports, alignment with CISA water-sector guidance; owner sign-off per site.

Medium

Three companies, three systems

CET, Horton and NuWave run different job-cost and payroll stacks.

Hedge: build the data layer first; defer ERP consolidation past year one.

Medium

Slow municipal contracting

Assure and SCADA monitoring contracts move at council speed.

Hedge: offer monitoring as a priced O&M option at close-out of SRF-funded projects.

Medium

Over-claiming the multiple

Re-rating is the least certain lever; the typical-to-premium spread is only ~3 turns in total.

Hedge: underwrite on EBITDA; treat 0.5–1.5 turns as upside, not the base case.

Low

Vendor lock-in & pricing

Volume-based pricing grows with backlog; long monitoring contracts limit switching.

Hedge: own the data layer and asset registry; negotiate multi-entity terms as add-ons join.

07Why a buyer pays for this

From project contractor to service platform.

At exit, GridOS is the evidence: contracted recurring revenue, visible backlog, and a repeatable way to plug in the next acquisition.
