02 · A day of calls

The leaks cluster when nobody is at the desk

What happens today
  • After 5 p.m. and weekends: voicemail or a message service. Trades lose 30–40% of these calls.
  • Lunch and storm days: reps are busy, so about 12% of daytime calls ring out (assumption).
  • Callers ring the next company on Google.
With 24/7 voice coverage
  • Every call answered on ring one; the agent says it is the virtual assistant.
  • Safety screen, ServiceTitan lookup, open capacity, booking.
  • Hazards, VIPs and complaints warm-transfer with a summary.
