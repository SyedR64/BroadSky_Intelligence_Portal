02 A day of calls

The leaks cluster when nobody is at the desk

About 115 calls a day reach Punctual Pros' brand lines (est.). Here is when they arrive, how many go unanswered today, and what the same day looks like when an AI agent picks up every call on ring one.

What happens today

  • After 5 p.m. and on weekends, calls go to voicemail or an answering service that takes a message. Trades lose 30–40% of these calls.
  • At lunch and on storm days, CSRs are already on the phone, so about 12% of daytime calls ring out or are abandoned (assumption).
  • The caller hangs up and calls the next company listed on Google.

With 24/7 voice coverage

  • Every call is answered on the first ring with the brand greeting, the AI disclosure and the recording notice.
  • The agent runs the safety screen, finds the customer in ServiceTitan, offers open capacity and books the job.
  • CSRs stay on the calls they are already handling. Hazards, VIPs and complaints are warm-transferred with a summary.

    Design rationale

    Why this page looks and works the way it does

    It is written for Tyler and the PRG. The page has to make the case in 90 seconds and then survive diligence on every input.

    A clock instead of a slide

    A debt-clock-style counter makes the cost of waiting concrete. It is labelled as a modelled estimate for Punctual Pros, not a measured loss, and its formula is one click away. That framing means the debate is about the inputs, not about the pitch.

    Hear it before you believe it

    Partners decide on voice AI by listening to it. The transcript player uses the browser's own speech engine, so the demo runs with no vendor account and no audio files. The tool rail shows that the agent books real jobs and does not just take messages.

    Benchmarked like EliseAI, built like Twilio

    The page measures the program the way EliseAI does, by booked jobs and after-hours share rather than call-center cost. It shows the engineering the way Twilio does, with a latency budget for every hop. Those are the two questions a buyer asks at exit.

    Calculator with presets

    The conservative model is the default. Presets switch to Invoca's industry miss rate or ServiceTitan's AI booking rate, so the range of outcomes is visible and nobody has to argue about one number.

    Governance on the page

    The TCPA, AI-disclosure and all-party recording rules are stated next to the revenue. Diligence will ask about them, and they constrain outbound more than inbound.

    KPIs this program should move

    After-hours answer rate and booking rate, overflow abandon rate, time to book, membership renewal rate, the escalation rate (10–20% in month 1, then falling) and booked revenue per inbound call.
