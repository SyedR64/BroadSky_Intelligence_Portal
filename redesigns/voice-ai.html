02 · A day of calls

The leaks cluster when nobody is at the desk

About 115 calls a day reach Punctual Pros' brand linesest.. Here is when they arrive, how many go unanswered today, and what the same day looks like when an AI agent picks up every call on ring one.

What happens today
  • After 5 p.m. and on weekends, calls go to voicemail or an answering service that takes a message. Trades lose 30–40% of these calls.
  • At lunch and on storm days, customer service reps are already on the phone, so about 12% of daytime calls ring out or are abandoned (assumption).
  • The caller hangs up and calls the next company listed on Google.
With 24/7 voice coverage
  • Every call is answered on the first ring with the brand greeting, and the agent says it is the virtual assistant.
  • The agent runs the safety screen, finds the customer in ServiceTitan, offers open capacity and books the job.
  • Reps stay on the calls they are already handling. Hazards, VIPs and complaints are warm-transferred with a summary.

      Design rationale

      Why this page looks and works the way it does

      It is written for the Broad Sky Portfolio Resource Group. The page has to make the case in 90 seconds and then survive diligence on every input.

      A clock instead of a slide

      A running counter makes the cost of waiting concrete. It is labelled as a modelled estimate for Punctual Pros, not a measured loss, and its formula is one click away, so the debate is about the inputs, not the pitch.

      Hear it before you believe it

      Partners decide on voice AI by listening to it. The player uses the browser's own speech engine, so the demo runs with no vendor account and no audio files. The call log shows that the agent books real jobs and does not just take messages.

      Measure outcomes, show the engineering

      The program is measured by booked jobs and after-hours share rather than call-center cost, and the engineering is shown with a latency budget for every hop. Those are the two questions a buyer asks at exit.

      Calculator with presets

      The conservative model is the default. Presets switch to Invoca's industry miss rate or ServiceTitan's AI booking rate, so the range of outcomes is visible and nobody has to argue about one number.

      Guardrails on the page

      Handoff times, call scoring and change control sit next to the revenue, because a buyer will ask how quality holds up as volume grows.

      KPIs this program should move

      After-hours answer and booking rates, overflow abandon rate, time to book, membership renewal rate, the escalation rate (10–20% in month one, then falling) and booked revenue per inbound call.
