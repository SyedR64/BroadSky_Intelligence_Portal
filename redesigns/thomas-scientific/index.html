Design rationale

What we borrowed, why it converts, and which KPI it should move

Thomas Scientific sits in a low-growth, scale-margin category: public lab and healthcare distributors grew a median 1.5% in their latest year at a 5.0% median operating margin (public_comps, SEC filings). Volume will not carry the equity story, so this site is designed to cut cost-to-serve and win share of wallet: search first, reorder in two clicks, and turn every visit into a quote, a punchout connection or a VMI conversation.
