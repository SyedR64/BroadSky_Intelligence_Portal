LabOS · the tech-enablement thesis for Thomas Scientific

Make every reorder digital.
Make every account visible.

LabOS is the operating layer for a 125-year-old distributor: AI search that understands specs and CAS numbers, punchout into the customer's procurement system, vendor-managed inventory, and account-health scoring across 27,503 mapped US lab sites. Built mostly from proven vendors, with a thin Broad Sky data layer on top.

What it is

Six modules, one operating layer.

Each module replaces a phone call, a spreadsheet or a sales visit with a workflow the customer runs inside their own systems.

The e-commerce penetration thesis

Volume is flat. Digital share is not.

Lab distribution grows in the low single digits. The distributors gaining share are the ones whose customers order digitally, inside their own procurement systems.
