Growth plan · for the Broad Sky deal team and the Portfolio Resource Group (PRG) · Oct 2026

From distributor to lab supply-chain partner.

Reading as

Thomas is Broad Sky's first deal (BSP-TS, LP, closed Dec 2021) and its most stressed position. The plan puts cash and the December 2027 maturity first and growth second, but the growth story is what earns the refinancing: target-account selling into 27,503 scored labs and health-system sites, digital and punchout ordering, private label, specialty verticals, AI agents that release cash, and two tuck-ins that happen only once they are funded.

01 · The template

Smith + Howard went from one office to a TPG exit. Thomas runs a different version of the same play.

Each Smith + Howard move is dated and sourced; next to it is what that move means for a 125-year-old lab distributor. The Thomas version of "national scale" is not 4x revenue. It is an amend-and-extend by the December 2027 maturity, then a refinanceable, re-rated company by month 36 (Oct 2029).

    03 · Eight levers

    Margin comes from mix, coverage and cash, not from a market growing 1.5% a year.

    Each lever has a sourced benchmark and a target. Where Thomas's own baseline is not disclosed the bar shows a hatched gap: those are the first 30 days of diligence.

    04 · The AI layer

    Nine agents that sell, key, search, forecast and collect.

    Named vendors, a published result each and weeks to deploy. Collections and pricing go first because each day of DSO is worth about $0.8M before the refinancing.

    05 · Talent

    Thirty account books, a specialist bench and an onsite corps.

    The target-account program is a people program first: a Grainger-style inside-sales academy, faster ramps, apprenticeships for the DCs and VMI sites, and AI that moves customer-service reps from keying orders to selling.

    07 · Financing and returns

    Two steps: an amend-and-extend in 2027 sized on ~$33M of EBITDA, then a refinancing or sale near 6x at month 36.

    The loan matures 14 Dec 2027, month 14 of the plan. Month 36 (Oct 2029) comes after it, so the growth case cannot be the refinancing case: the 2027 deal is sized on the EBITDA Thomas can show by then, and the gap is closed with cash levers plus sponsor preferred, paydown or lender terms.         09 · Method

        How this page was built, and what sits behind it.

        The design choices, then every dataset and link the numbers come from.

        Design rationale · why this page looks and reads the way it does

        Credit memo first, growth deck second

        Written the way lender and restructuring memos are: the maturity countdown and loan-mark sparkline sit in the first screen. Thomas's story only works if the reader sees that every lever is sequenced behind liquidity. KPI moved: quality of the 2027 amend-and-extend conversation.

        Report in the units buyers benchmark

        Digital share, ePro share and specialist ratios are the headline metrics. Public lab and industrial distributors (Avantor, Grainger) disclose them, and they are the numbers a strategic or sponsor buyer will benchmark Thomas against, so the plan reports in the same units. KPIs moved: digital share of transactions, private-label mix.

        Lead with the number

        A KPI-first hero, one accent colour on a calm canvas, crisp cards and interactive sliders. A Portfolio Resource Group partner can pressure-test the multiple, premium and debt assumptions live in an IC or lender meeting instead of asking for a new model.

        The Smith + Howard exit release

        The dated entry-to-exit timeline. Mapping each move onto Thomas shows where the analogy holds (specialist add-ons, tech and offshore) and where it does not (4x revenue), which makes the plan more credible, not less.
