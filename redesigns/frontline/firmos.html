What it is

Six modules on one data layer.

FirmOS builds on HELIX, the AI-optimized desk Frontline launched at ILTACON in August 2025, and on the PSA, RMM and eBilling tools Frontline already runs. It adds the layer that connects them and shows the results to the client.

Tier-0 AI desk

Resolves password, MFA, access, DMS and printer requests in seconds. It answers from each firm's runbooks and hands off to a human with full context.

Moves: first-level resolution · cost per ticket

Posture

Continuous control monitoring that produces a live security score and client-ready evidence for outside counsel guidelines and cyber insurers.

Moves: security attach · renewal rate

LEDES pre-flight

Checks every invoice line against the client's billing guidelines, UTBMS codes and rate caps before it reaches the client's e-billing portal.

Moves: rejection rate · realization

Collect

Ranks A/R by risk, drafts appeals for rejected lines, and automates cash application across bank, billing system and portal.

Moves: days to pay · write-offs

Client portal

Tickets, SLAs, security posture and the billing pipeline in one view built for the firm's COO, CIO and CFO.

Moves: self-serve rate · NPS

Insight

Quarterly review packs assembled from the data, plus early warnings when a client's usage or sentiment points to churn risk.

Moves: gross churn <10% · upsell

Evidence behind the multiple

From serviceos_evidence.json, items that apply to Frontline
Build vs buy

Buy the plumbing. Build the layer clients see.

Commodity capabilities come from proven vendors Frontline can price today. The proprietary layer is the data model, the Tier-0 agent on top of HELIX, the scoring and the portal. That layer is what a buyer will diligence.

12-month roadmap

Value in two quarters, platform in four.

The plan is sequenced so the first EBITDA comes from the desk, which is fast and measurable. The second comes from the revenue cycle. The growth motion starts once the posture score is live in the portal.

Risks

What could go wrong, and the control for each.

Law firms buy on trust. Each risk below has a named mitigation that ships with the module, not after it.

Why a buyer pays for this at exit

Proprietary operating data is what turns a services company into a platform.

Margin a buyer can underwrite

Tier-0 deflection and Level 1 resolution are measured per ticket, per client, every month. The EBITDA bridge is auditable, not an add-back.

Retention with evidence

Posture scores and quarterly review packs give clients a reason to renew. Gross churn held under 10% is the threshold MSP buyers watch (Aventis).

A repeatable growth engine

An assessment-led motion into 143 researched mid-size firms, with fit scores, shows organic growth that doesn't depend on M&A.

Security and RCM attach

Security is the top revenue line for high-margin MSPs (Kaseya 2025). Revenue-cycle services are legal-specific and hard for generalist MSPs to copy.
