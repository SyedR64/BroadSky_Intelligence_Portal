Value-creation playbook · for Tyler Zachem & the PRG · Oct 2026

From half the AM Law 200 to a global legal-operations platform.

Frontline already has what Smith + Howard spent its hold building: national reach, an offshore delivery base and about half the AM Law 200. If entry was in the 12-15x EBITDA range that public filings imply, the return has to come from EBITDA growth rather than multiple expansion. The plan is to earn more from each firm, move into the mid-market, roll up revenue-cycle services, then enter the UK and Canada by acquiring local providers.

          Design rationale · why this page looks and reads the way it does

          Stripe and Ramp product pages

          Borrowed: a hero that leads with KPIs, and one violet accent on a calm ink-and-paper canvas. The four numbers Tyler gets asked about (revenue, EBITDA, people, law-firm clients) are on the first screen, so the page works as a 30-second read before an IC or lender call. KPI moved: time to an IC-ready plan.

          The Smith + Howard exit release

          Borrowed: the entry → exit framing on a dated, sourced timeline. Following the structure of a Broad Sky playbook that has already exited makes the Frontline plan easier to trust for the PRG, the lenders (NXT, Audax) and a future buyer. KPI moved: confidence in the add-on budget and the co-invest ask.

          Legal-ops peers (Consilio, Integreon, Clio)

          Borrowed: a sober navy palette, a monospaced data voice and maps that show coverage. A buyer of a legal-operations platform wants to see density, recurring revenue and an international leg, not a list of logos. KPIs moved: add-ons closed a year, international revenue share, recurring mix.

          A conversational front door

          The "Ask the playbook" assistant answers from the same datasets as the page. Tyler or an LP can ask "which targets come first?" mid-meeting instead of scrolling. KPI moved: time to answer in a live discussion.
