Growth plan · Oct 2026

From half the AM Law 200 to a global legal-operations company.

Frontline already has what Smith + Howard spent its hold building: national reach, an offshore delivery base and about half the AM Law 200. If entry was in the 12–15x EBITDA range that public filings imply, the return has to come from EBITDA growth rather than multiple expansion. The plan is to earn more from each firm, move into the mid-market, roll up revenue-cycle services, then enter the UK and Canada by acquiring local providers.

          Design rationale · why this page looks and reads the way it does

          Lead with the number

          A hero that leads with KPIs, and one accent on a calm ink-and-paper canvas. The four numbers the PRG gets asked about (revenue, EBITDA, people, law-firm clients) are on the first screen, so the page works as a 30-second read before an investment-committee or lender call. KPI moved: time to an IC-ready plan.

          Entry to exit on a dated timeline

          The entry → exit framing on a dated, sourced timeline. Following a Broad Sky growth plan that has already exited (Smith + Howard) makes the Frontline plan easier to trust for the PRG, the lenders (NXT, Audax) and a future buyer. KPI moved: confidence in the add-on budget and the co-invest ask.

          Show coverage, not logos

          A sober palette, a consistent data voice and maps that show coverage. A buyer of a legal-operations company wants to see density, recurring revenue and an international leg, not a list of logos. KPIs moved: add-ons closed a year, international revenue share, recurring mix.

          Answers on demand

          The "Ask the plan" assistant answers from the same datasets as the page. A partner or an LP can ask "which targets come first?" mid-meeting instead of scrolling. KPI moved: time to answer in a live discussion.
