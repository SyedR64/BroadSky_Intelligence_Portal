    Design rationale

    Frontline sells to law-firm COOs, CIOs and CFOs who buy through security reviews and multi-stakeholder committees. The site works as a trust center, a self-serve calculator and an editorial brand, in that order. Every element below is borrowed from a live reference site reviewed on 2026-10-06, and each one is tied to a KPI.

    Assessment requests / monthPrimary conversion. Self-assessment completions should convert to requests at 8–12% est.
    Security-review cycle timeTrust panel plus advisor Q&A should cut days from questionnaire to signature.
    Portal self-serve rateUtility-bar portal links move existing clients to self-serve, lowering cost per ticket.
    Cross-sell attachShowing IT, security and RCM as one service lifts multi-line clients, the base for a platform multiple.
