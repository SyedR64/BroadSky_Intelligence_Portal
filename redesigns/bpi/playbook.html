Design rationale: why this page looks like this

FGS Global (KKR): a memo, not a brochure

FGS sells like an advisory firm, not an agency: plain statements and monospace labels. The memo header and numbered sections borrow that, so Tyler can read the page as an investment-committee document. KPI it should move: time to decision on the PRG asks.

Penta (Shamrock): intelligence as the product

Penta lists "Intelligence" first and keeps a persistent product CTA. The SignalOS button in the nav and the CTA strip do the same job, keeping the productized recurring layer, the source of the multiple premium, in view at every scroll depth. KPI: recurring share of net revenue.

Precision (Abry): bold claims, then proof

Precision pairs bold two-tone headlines with evidence. Here every headline sits next to a sourced figure or an est. label, so the PRG can tell analyst assumptions from filings at a glance. KPI: fewer diligence follow-ups and faster lender and buyer Q&A at exit.
