02 — What it is

Six modules.
One subscription.

Buy the commodity layers from proven vendors, build the parts that make BPI different: the narrative models, the Message House panels, the compliance rules and the client scorecards.

Narrative MonitorNews, social, video, podcasts and dockets clustered into narratives, scored for sentiment, velocity and synthetic-media risk. Alerts arrive with a draft holding statement.Buy Meltwater · Brandwatch · Zignal, build models
Audience LabSynthetic pre-tests against policymaker, investor, voter and employee segments in hours, then live validation with Message House elite panels.Build on Message House
Compliance StudioDrafts statements, op-eds and Hill letters in each client’s voice, then routes them through claims, disclosure and legal checkpoints before anything ships.Buy Jasper / Writer, build rules
Policy RadarBills, rulemakings and EU acts tracked per client in Washington, Brussels and Berlin, with impact summaries and outreach variants.Buy Quorum / FiscalNote
Outcome DashboardsShare of voice, narrative sentiment, policy outcomes and paid-media lift in one scorecard per retainer, so renewals are sold on results.Build (BI layer)
Retainer EngineCompares hours burned with scope every week, flags over-servicing and drafts change orders, protecting margin as the retainer share rises.Buy PSA, build alerts

    08 — Risks

    What could go wrong,
    and the guardrail.

    Communications firms sell trust. Every SignalOS module keeps a human in the loop, and the commercial risks are managed as deliberately as the technical ones.

    HighRevenue stays tied to the election cycle

    Subscriptions sold mostly to campaign clients would churn with the cycle.

    Guardrail: sell first to corporate clients from the Reputation Resilience Index briefings; report recurring share monthly.

    HighAn AI error reaches a client statement

    A hallucinated figure in a crisis statement would cost more than the product earns.

    Guardrail: approved fact base, claims and disclosure checks, two human gates, full audit log, no auto-publish.

    MediumClients build it themselves

    Writer’s commissioned Forrester study reports a 50% cut in external agency scope for in-house AI users.

    Guardrail: productize first and sell what clients can’t buy: Message House panels, transatlantic counsel, outcome scorecards.

    MediumConfidentiality and data protection

    Client drafts and stakeholder maps are sensitive; EU clients need GDPR-grade handling.

    Guardrail: SOC 2-aligned vendors, EU data residency for Berlin and Brussels accounts, per-client workspaces.

    MediumVendor lock-in and cost creep

    Monitoring licences reprice at renewal and usage-based pricing scales with mentions.

    Guardrail: two monitoring vendors, BPI-owned data layer and scoring models, renegotiate at month 12.

    LowSynthetic research credibility

    Clients and reporters may question simulated audiences.

    Guardrail: synthetic tests only narrow options; finalists are always validated with live panels and the method is published.
