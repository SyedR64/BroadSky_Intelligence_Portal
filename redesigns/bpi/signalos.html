What it is

Six modules. One subscription.

Buy the commodity layers. Build what makes BPI different: narrative models, research panels, review rules and client scorecards.

Narrative monitor

News, social, video, podcasts and dockets clustered into narratives, scored for sentiment, velocity and synthetic-media risk. Alerts arrive with a draft holding statement.

Buy Meltwater, Brandwatch, Zignal · build models

Audience lab

Synthetic pre-tests against policymaker, investor, voter and employee segments in hours, then live validation with Message House elite panels.

Build on Message House

Compliance studio

Drafts statements, op-eds and Hill letters in each client’s voice, then routes them through claims, disclosure and legal checkpoints before anything ships.

Buy Jasper or Writer · build rules

Policy radar

Bills, rulemakings and EU acts tracked per client in Washington, Brussels and Berlin, with impact summaries and outreach variants.

Buy Quorum or FiscalNote

Outcome dashboards

Share of voice, narrative sentiment, policy outcomes and paid-media lift in one scorecard per retainer, so renewals are sold on results.

Retainer engine

Compares hours burned with scope every week, flags over-servicing and drafts change orders, protecting margin as the retainer share rises.

    Risks

    What could go wrong, and the guardrail.

    Communications firms sell trust. Every SignalOS module keeps a person in the loop.

    High

    Revenue stays tied to the election cycle

    Subscriptions sold mostly to campaign clients would churn with the cycle.

    Guardrail. Sell first to corporate clients from the Reputation Resilience Index briefings; report recurring share monthly.
    High

    An AI error reaches a client statement

    A hallucinated figure in a crisis statement would cost more than the product earns.

    Guardrail. Approved fact base, claims and disclosure checks, two human gates, full audit log, no auto-publish.
    Medium

    Clients build it themselves

    Writer’s commissioned Forrester study reports a 50% cut in external agency scope for in-house AI users.

    Guardrail. Productize first and sell what clients cannot buy: Message House panels, transatlantic counsel, outcome scorecards.
    Medium

    Confidentiality and data protection

    Client drafts and stakeholder maps are sensitive; EU clients need GDPR-grade handling.

    Guardrail. SOC 2-aligned vendors, EU data residency for Berlin and Brussels accounts, per-client workspaces.
    Medium

    Vendor lock-in and cost creep

    Monitoring licences reprice at renewal and usage-based pricing scales with mentions.

    Guardrail. Two monitoring vendors, a BPI-owned data layer and scoring models, renegotiate at month 12.
    Low

    Synthetic research credibility

    Clients and reporters may question simulated audiences.

    Guardrail. Synthetic tests only narrow options; finalists are always validated with live panels and the method is published.
