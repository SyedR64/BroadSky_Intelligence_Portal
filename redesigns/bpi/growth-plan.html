Design rationale: why this page looks like this

A memo, not a brochure

Advisory firms sell with plain statements and monospace labels, not agency gloss. The memo header and numbered sections follow that principle, so the Portfolio Resource Group can read the page as an investment-committee document. KPI it should move: time to decision on the PRG asks.

Intelligence as the product

Keep the product one click away at all times. The SignalOS button in the page bar and the closing call to action do the same job, keeping the productized recurring layer, the source of the multiple premium, in view at every scroll depth. KPI: recurring share of net revenue.

Bold claims, then proof

Pair every bold headline with evidence. Here every headline sits next to a sourced figure or an est. label, so the PRG can tell analyst assumptions from filings at a glance. KPI: fewer diligence follow-ups and faster lender and buyer Q&A at exit.
