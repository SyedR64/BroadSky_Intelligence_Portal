    08 — For Tyler and the Portfolio Resource Group

    Design rationale: what we borrowed, why it converts, what it should move

    OpenCollapse

    The system borrows from the three firms buyers will benchmark BPI against: a scaled PE-owned strategic-comms brand (FGS, KKR), a PE-backed DC peer that sells intelligence as a product (Penta, Shamrock), and BPI’s closest campaign-born rival (Precision, Abry). The goal is a site that prices like an advisory, sells like a product company and reads as diversified beyond the election cycle.

    FGS Global fgsglobal.com ↗

    KKR majority owner; WPP stake sold at a ~$1.7B EV
    • Monospace nav + statement headlineCounsel-grade gravitas that supports retainer pricing above project-agency rates.
    • Region awareness (live office clocks)Our stand-in for FGS’s region switcher: makes London, Brussels and Berlin visible, where cross-border mandates carry higher fees.
    • Transaction & financial comms namedOpens a PE-sponsor channel for deal and portfolio communications.

    Penta pentagroup.co ↗

    Acquired by Shamrock Capital, Aug 2025
    • Named live product in the primary nav“Penta live” becomes “SignalOS live”: productized monitoring creates subscription-like revenue inside an agency.
    • Intelligence shown as a working productThe narrative monitor replaces stock photos and leads with data, which separates BPI from creative-led shops.
    • Big-number bandFast scale proof for corporate-affairs buyers.
