Growth plan · Oct 2026

From Lancaster to national.

Broad Sky took Smith + Howard from one Atlanta office to 11 locations, nine add-ons and ~4x revenue in 3.5 years, then sold it to TPG Growth. Punctual Pros can run the same play: densify Central PA, repeat the Horvath deal across the Mid-Atlantic, and become the national consolidator inside the Authority Brands system. The new website is the front door. Behind it sit AI agents, programs for the technicians and a tuck-in machine.

        Design rationale · why this page looks and reads the way it does

        Lead with the number

        A KPI-first hero and one accent colour on a calm canvas. It puts the four numbers the deal team will be asked about (revenue, EBITDA, technicians, territories) in the first screen, so the page works as a 30-second read before an IC or lender call.

        Entry to exit on a dated timeline

        The "entry → exit" framing with a dated, sourced timeline. Following the structure of a growth plan that already produced a Broad Sky exit (Smith + Howard) makes the Punctual Pros plan credible to the PRG, lenders and a future buyer. KPI moved: time to an IC-ready plan.

        Show density on a map

        Route-density maps. A map of real ZIP codes and real targets shows a buyer that the company is a density story, not a list of logos. KPIs moved: tuck-ins closed per year, revenue per territory.

        Answers on demand

        The "Ask the plan" box with autocomplete lets a deal partner or an LP ask "which counties come first?" and get an answer computed from the same datasets as the page, which is faster than scrolling. KPI moved: time to answer during a live meeting.
