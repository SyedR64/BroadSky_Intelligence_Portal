Value-creation playbook · prepared for Tyler Zachem & the PRG · Oct 2026

From Lancaster
to national.

Broad Sky took Smith + Howard from one Atlanta office to 11 locations, nine add-ons and ~4x revenue in 3.5 years, then sold it to TPG Growth. Punctual Pros can run the same play: densify Central PA, repeat the Horvath deal across the Mid-Atlantic, and become the national consolidator inside the Authority Brands system. The new website is the front door. Behind it sit AI agents, programs for the technicians and a tuck-in machine.

Today = Oct 2026 run-rate. Month 36 = plan. Territories = franchised outlets (one per brand per area). All figures est. analyst assumptions anchored to public filings (PGIM 10-Q, FDD Item 19, Form D), not company guidance.

    02 · Where we go, in order

    Four phases, from 248 core zips to a national franchise system.

    Core zips, Tier-1 adjacent zips and every screened add-on target are plotted from the portal's datasets. Pick a phase, or press play.

    03 · Organic growth

    Ways we improve the business. The website is only one of them.

    Each lever has a sourced benchmark, a baseline and a target. Where Punctual Pros' own number is not public we say so, so the first 30 days of diligence can fill it.

    04 · The AI layer

    AI agents for the office, the truck and the customer.

    Eleven agents from named vendors, each with a published benchmark and weeks to deploy. Each card says what its number is: a result, a survey or the size of the problem. They go live in the order on the runway, fastest payback first.

    05 · Helping the pros

    Demand is not the constraint. Technicians are.

    The plan treats HVAC techs, plumbers, electricians and partner contractors as the scarce asset: recruit them earlier, pay them on a ladder, and give them AI copilots so juniors are productive sooner.

    06 · The tuck-in machine

    Ten founders to call first, and what happens on day one.

    Ranked from the portal's add-on screen (fit score out of 100: density, trade mix, scale, ownership readiness, brand alignment).

    07 · Financing & returns

    How we pay for it, and what it is worth at the end.

    Capital comes from the existing unitranche, seller rollover, Fund I and a deal-level co-invest. Value comes from organic EBITDA growth, acquired EBITDA and multiple arbitrage. An AI premium is shown only as an upside case, never in the base. Every figure is labelled est.

        Design rationale · why this page looks and reads the way it does

        Stripe / Ramp product pages

        Borrowed: a KPI-first hero and one accent colour on a calm canvas. It puts the four numbers Tyler will be asked about (revenue, EBITDA, technicians, territories) in the first screen, so the page works as a 30-second read before an IC or lender call.

        PE value-creation memos (Smith + Howard exit release)

        Borrowed: the "entry → exit" framing with a dated, sourced timeline. Copying the structure of a playbook that already produced an exit makes the Punctual Pros plan credible to the PRG, lenders and a future buyer. KPI moved: time to an IC-ready plan.

        Peer home-services platforms (Sila, Wrench, Legacy)

        Borrowed: a navy-and-orange trades palette and route-density maps. A map of real zips and real targets shows a buyer that the platform is a density story, not a list of logos. KPIs moved: tuck-ins closed per year, revenue per territory.

        Conversational front door

        The "Ask the playbook" box with autocomplete lets Tyler or an LP ask "which counties come first?" and get an answer computed from the same datasets as the page, which is faster than scrolling. KPI moved: time to answer during a live meeting.
