  • Local Services Ads base: $39–57 per lead at a ~44% book rate (SearchLight, Feb 2026); ranked by reviews and answer speed.
  • ZIP-targeted CTV: bought by ZIP, so spend stays near the Punctual Pros footprint.
  • New-mover mail: arm’s-length home buyers, a floor, from the county deed feeds already in the portal.
  • Weather triggers: a National Weather Service warning for a Punctual Pros county adds 50% to CTV for 72 hours and puts the storm cut live. It re-times spend: ~$5k a year at Phase 1 weights.
  • Terms: LSA, Google Local Services Ads. CTV, connected TV. VO, voiceover. ROAS, return on ad spend. DMA, a TV market area. NWS, National Weather Service.
