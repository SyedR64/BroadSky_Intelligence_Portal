Growth marketing · Oct 2026

ZIP-targeted TV, not the whole market.

Sell the punctuality promise the three brands already own. Google Local Services Ads are the always-on base. ZIP-targeted connected TV, starting in the 61 highest-priority ZIP codes, builds familiarity before the furnace fails. Postcards go to every new homeowner the deed feed finds. National Weather Service alerts move budget 12–36 hours before calls spike.

      05 · Where to buy affordable CTV

      Twelve ways onto the TV screen, compared on minimums, CPM and ZIP targeting.

      ZIP targeting decides it. A market-wide buy in Harrisburg–Lancaster–Lebanon–York (~2.1M people) puts most impressions outside Punctual Pros’ ZIP codes, and a New York or Philadelphia market buy for the shore puts more than 90% outside.

      06 · Test plan

      Prove it in Central PA before scaling: LSA and search decided at week 8, CTV at week 12.

      Each channel gets its own call-tracking number in ServiceTitan. Tier-I ZIP codes are paired and one of each pair is held dark from CTV; 20% of new movers are held out from mail. The KPI is cost per incremental booked job. The power table shows what each read can and cannot detect. Completion rate is not a KPI: CTV is non-skippable, so it runs 94–99% regardless of the ad.

      Design rationale · for the Portfolio Resource Group (PRG)

      Why the page is built this way.

      Live numbers and a working toolShow the product working instead of describing it. Here that is the hero KPIs, the calculator and the live weather feed, so the PRG tests the media plan rather than reading claims about it. Moves: time to a budget decision, and confidence in the cost-per-job assumptions.
      Every touch ends in an actionPhone, rating, booking and membership belong on every page and every spot. In the sample ads, every creative ends in a trackable QR or number and a membership path. Moves: book rate, membership attach (25% target) and lead-source attribution in ServiceTitan.
      Speak to the weatherWeather urgency drives emergency demand. The trigger engine does this automatically from weather alerts, with the guarantee as the reason to call Punctual Pros first. Moves: share of storm-day calls, LSA impression share in surge weeks and cost per booked job during alerts.
      Why it matters at exitA buyer pays more for growth it can underwrite. Channel-level acquisition cost, geo-holdout lift and payback, kept in one place, become a documented customer-acquisition engine for the data room. Without them, marketing is just an expense line.
