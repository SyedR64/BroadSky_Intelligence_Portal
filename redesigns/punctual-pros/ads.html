      Design rationale

      Why the page is built this way.

      Live numbers and a working toolShow the plan working, not described. Moves: time to a budget decision.
      Every touch ends in an actionEvery creative ends in a trackable number and a membership path. Moves: book rate and attribution.
      Speak to the weatherWeather drives emergency demand, so spend follows the alerts. Moves: share of storm-day calls.
      Why it matters at exitA buyer pays more for growth it can underwrite. Measured cost per job turns marketing into a documented acquisition engine.
