TV for PP’s zips,
not the whole DMA.

Sell the punctuality promise the three brands already own. Google Local Services Ads are the always-on base. Zip-targeted connected TV, starting in the 61 highest-priority zips, builds familiarity before the furnace fails. Postcards go to every new homeowner the deed feed finds. NWS alerts move budget 12–36 hours before calls spike.

Phase 1 (Central PA, Nov 2026 – Apr 2027), conservative case. Every figure is est.: modelled from cited benchmarks plus labelled assumptions in data/research/pp_ads.json, to be replaced by the test reads.

$39–57 per lead at a ~44% book rate (SearchLight, Feb 2026). Ranked by reviews and answer speed.

2Zip-targeted CTV

$50–500 self-serve minimums, ~$15–40 CPM. Starts in 61 Tier-I zips (336k homes). Bought by zip, so spend stays close to PP’s footprint; IP-to-zip matching is imperfect, so check each vendor’s delivery-by-zip report.

3New-mover mail

~2,300 PA and ~1,150 NJ arm’s-length home buyers a month (floor), from the county deed feeds already in the portal.

4Weather triggers

NWS warning for a PP county → +50% CTV for 72 h and the storm cut goes live. Re-times spend; ~$5k a year at Phase 1 weights.

      Design rationale · for the PRG

      Why the page is built this way

      Ramp: live numbers and a working tool

      Ramp sells with a running counter and a product you can use on the homepage. Here that is the hero KPIs, the calculator and the live NWS feed, so the PRG tests the media plan rather than reading claims about it. Moves: time to a budget decision, and confidence in the CPA assumptions.

      ramp.com

      Parker & Sons (Wrench Group): conversion funnel

      Wrench brands put phone, rating, booking and membership on every page. In the sample ads, every creative ends in a trackable QR or number and a membership path. Moves: book rate, membership attach (25% target), and lead-source attribution in ServiceTitan.

      parkerandsons.com

      Frank Gay (Apex): storm and heat language

      Frank Gay uses weather urgency to drive emergency demand. The trigger engine does this automatically from NWS alerts, with the guarantee as the reason to call PP first. Moves: share of storm-day calls, LSA impression share in surge weeks, and cost per booked job during alerts.

      frankgayservices.com

      Why it matters at exit

      A buyer pays more for growth it can underwrite. Channel-level CAC, geo-holdout lift and payback, kept in one place, become a documented customer-acquisition engine for the data room. Without them, “marketing” is just an expense line.
