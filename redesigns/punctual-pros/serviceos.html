SERVICEOSTech-enablement thesis · Punctual Pros · Broad Sky Partners

Every truck in the right driveway before the phone rings.

ServiceOS is the operating layer for a 25-territory home-services franchisee: it forecasts call volume from the weather, books every call 24/7, routes the closest qualified tech, and turns one-time repairs into recurring members. Off-the-shelf where the market is mature, built where Broad Sky has the data edge.

What it is

Seven modules on one data spine.

The field-service system of record (ServiceTitan) is the spine. Every module reads from it and writes back to it, so the board, the members and the numbers in the data room stay in sync.

Build · BSP data edge

Service-Call Pressure Index

A 7-day forecast of call volume by hub and trade, from live weather and NWS alerts run through PP's weather-to-demand multipliers. Staffing, on-call and parts staging follow the index, not gut feel.

Moves: booked calls in peak weeks · overtime · same-day capacity Buy · ServiceTitan

Dispatch board

One board across Lancaster, York, Harrisburg and the Jersey Shore. Auto-assign by skill, distance and membership priority.

Moves: jobs / tech / day · on-time % Rent · Avoca

AI dispatcher

Answers every call at 2 a.m., quotes member pricing and books a real slot straight onto the board.

Moves: booking rate 38% → 59% Buy + build

Comfort Club engine

Attach at every call, auto-book the two tune-ups, renew by text, flag churn risk before the renewal date.

Moves: members / tech → 83 Build · deed feeds

New-mover autopilot

County deed transfers → score → welcome mailer → opt-in call. New owners book within 90 days or not at all.

Moves: new-customer CAC · first-year members Buy · Profit Rhino + Rilla

In-home sales

Good / better / best on a tablet, financing at the kitchen table, and AI ride-along coaching on every replacement call.

Moves: avg ticket +6 pts · close rate +9% rel. Buy · Podium

Reviews & payments

Text-to-pay at job close, review request in the same thread, two-way SMS inbox for the CSR team.

Moves: review velocity · DSO

    Value-creation math

    $0.4–0.9M in, a multiple-point re-rate out.

    Revenue and EBITDA are estimates from public filings (pp_filings). Only 1.0–2.5 turns are credited to tech enablement because the 17–18.5x Champions and Sila prints are driven by scale. Every number below is labelled est. with its source.

    Assumptions est.

    pp_filings: ~$22M, range $16–28M (low–medium confidence)
    pp_filings: ~$1.8–3.1M, central ~$2.6M (low confidence)
    ra-pp: 2–4 pts (booking rate, ticket, membership)
    ve-06 Capstone/IMAP typical 6.8x · premium 9.8x
    ra-pp: 1.0–2.5 turns · ve-05 PKF 5–6x vs 10x+
    ra-pp: $0.4–0.9M software + implementation; 6–12 months to value

    Enterprise value bridge est.

    Bridge: EV today = EBITDA × multiple → + EBITDA uplift at today's multiple → + re-rating on the new EBITDA → − investment. Franchise royalties (~7.2–7.5% of revenue) cap margin upside (pp_filings). Analyst assumption, not a forecast or company guidance.

    Risks

    What could go wrong, and the mitigation.

    At exit

    Why a buyer pays for ServiceOS.

    PKF's valuation grid prices home-services businesses on a handful of rows. ServiceOS moves four of them at once, and leaves a data room that proves it.

    PKF row · In-house capabilities & innovation

    A platform, not a franchisee

    A documented operating system that onboards an add-on in 60 days is what turns a 25-territory operator into a buy-and-build platform for the next sponsor.

    Undifferentiated → Value-enhancing
    PKF row · Customer retention

    Members with a renewal history

    Cohort tables of members, renewals and churn by hub replace "we think retention is good." Recurring revenue gets capitalised; one-off repairs don't.

    Low retention → High retention
    PKF row · Business mix

    Service-led, not project-led

    Membership, tune-ups and repairs grow as a share of revenue versus lumpy replacements and new construction.

    Project-heavy (5–6x) → Repeatable service (10x+)
    PKF row · Earnings visibility

    A forecast you can underwrite

    The Pressure Index plus member book give a 7-day and 12-month demand view backed by NWS and ACS data, and a back-test against PP's own call history.

    Low visibility → High visibility
