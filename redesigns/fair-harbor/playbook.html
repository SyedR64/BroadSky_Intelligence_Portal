    Design rationale

    Why this page is built like a memo with a map.

    What it borrows, and why
      What it should move
      • Decision speed. One page, one thesis, four numbers in the hero, so the board can say yes or no to six asks in one meeting.
      • Legibility at exit. The S+H lesson is that buyers pay for a scaled, legible platform. Doors, repeat rate and margin are the three numbers a strategic buyer will diligence first.
      • Honesty about data. Every target carries an est. label and a source, because the baseline itself is a low-confidence estimate.
