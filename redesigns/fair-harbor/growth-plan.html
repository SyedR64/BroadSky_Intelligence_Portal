        Outcomes

        What it should move

        • Decision speed. One page, one thesis, four numbers in the hero, so the board can say yes or no to six asks in one meeting.
        • Legibility at exit. The Smith + Howard lesson is that buyers pay for a scaled, legible company. Doors, repeat rate and margin are the three numbers a strategic buyer will diligence first.
        • Honesty about data. Every target carries an est. label and a source, because the baseline itself is a low-confidence estimate.
