Concept Broad Sky tech-enablement thesis · Fair Harbor

The operating system for a brand people buy twice.

HarborOS puts AI fit and size, replenishment flows, a wholesale portal, season-aware forecasting and bottle-to-trunk traceability on one data layer. Fewer returns, more second orders, smarter buys, and a cleaner story at exit.

What it is

Six modules. One customer and SKU graph.

Mostly proven vendors, stitched together by a thin Broad Sky data layer. Every module is aimed at one of three margin leaks in DTC apparel: returns, one-and-done buyers, and inventory bought for the wrong size.

Foundation

Harbor data layer. Shopify Plus orders and catalog · Klaviyo profiles · returns reasons · wholesale sell-through · supplier certificates → one customer + SKU graph, owned by Fair Harbor and maintained by the Portfolio Resource Group.

Build vs buy

Buy the systems of record. Build the layer that compounds.

Named vendors and pricing notes come from the evidence file; confirm terms with each vendor before budgeting. Custom software is limited to the data layer, the fit-model tuning and the traceability ledger.

12-month roadmap

Live before the swim season, paid back by the next one.

Sequenced around the calendar that runs the business: fit and flows live before April, forecasting in time for in-season reorders, the wholesale portal ahead of the summer pre-book window. Time-to-value 4–9 months (est., ra-fh).
