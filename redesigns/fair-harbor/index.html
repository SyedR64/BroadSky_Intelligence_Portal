What's inside

Two technologies. One very good pair of trunks.

BreezeKnit liner

The scratchy mesh liner is the reason guys stop wearing swim trunks. BreezeKnit replaces it with a soft, stretchy knit that moves with you and dries fast, so the trunks go from the water to lunch without the chafe.

  • Anti-chafe knit, not mesh
  • In the Bayberry and Anchor
  • Built for all-day wear

Recycled-bottle shell

Post-consumer bottles are collected, washed, flaked, melted into pellets and spun into yarn, then woven into a quick-dry shell. About eleven bottles become one pair of trunks, and every pair carries the count on its care label.

  • ≈11 bottles per trunk
  • Quick-dry finish
  • Traceable lot on every label (HarborOS)

How we count (the formula)

bottles(t) = 27.0M + r × days since 1 Jan 2023

r = ($27M est. revenue × 40% swim share ÷ $88 median swim price × 11 bottles) ÷ 365

27M+ is the company-reported total on Fair Harbor's B Lab profile (certified June 2022), conservatively anchored at 1 Jan 2023. Revenue is the midpoint of a low-confidence estimate ($20–35M); the 40% swim share is an assumption; $88 is the median swim price on the live catalog (Sept 2026); 11 bottles per trunk is the brand's own ratio. What we deliberately did not copy

Allbirds opens with a "10% off" entry modal and a floating deals pill. With 38.5% of Fair Harbor's live variants already marked down (Sept 2026), training buyers to wait for a discount is the last thing the P&L needs. This site captures email with the fit guide instead.

Accessibility is a cost line

Fair Harbor has faced two ADA web-accessibility suits in S.D.N.Y. (2024, 2026). This concept is built to WCAG 2.2 AA: landmarks, skip link, visible focus, labelled controls, reduced-motion support and 4.5:1 text contrast. That closes off a recurring legal cost.

One system, not a skin

Every interactive module (fit, bottle counter, wholesale, replenishment) is a HarborOS surface, so the site doubles as the demo of the value-creation thesis. See the math.
