Concept: the portfolio as a film

Six scenes render the portal's own datasets on the GPU, over vector tiles with 3D terrain. Each scene answers one question a partner would ask:
  1. Portfolio: where the platforms sit and how big each footprint is.
  2. Where the homes trade: every recorded home sale in the Punctual Pros territory, with the newest 90 days pulsing.
  3. Nationwide: the phased path from East Hempfield to the Sun Belt, plus screened add-on targets.
  4. New England grid: CET opportunities and the wastewater plants Horton opens up.
  5. Storm: live NWS alerts over the territory and the hubs that staff to them.
  6. Close: every layer at once.
Hover a column, disc or marker for details. Figures are labelled "est." where they are estimates. Public and licensed data: verify before use.
