# Broad Sky OS launch film

Working papers for a 3 to 7 minute film that introduces Broad Sky Partners and Broad Sky OS, to be sent privately to the firm. The film has not been made. The two films already in `briefing/` (the 2:13 cinematic film and the 5:03 video briefing) are older and are not re-rendered.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `SCRIPT.md` | Draft 2 of the script, dated 23 November 2026: narration, a 37-shot list, a style guide, prompts for every generated shot, the numbers to re-check, what is ready to record, what is not yet, and a production plan with 75-second and 25-second cuts. Target length 4:30. |
| `research_video_tools.md` | The state of AI video generation in late November 2026: tools, limits, prices, prompting order for the two chosen tools, and cautions when a film shows a real firm. Every figure carries its source; items marked UNVERIFIED were not confirmed on the maker's page. |
| `research_yc_films.md` | How start-up launch films are built: structure beat by beat, voice, how the product is shown, pacing, what reads as cheap, and the beat sheet the script follows. |

The script was written against the product as it stood on 23 November 2026. Section 8 of the script lists every number the narration uses and where the product shows it; re-check each one on the day the voice is recorded. Section 10 lists the product changes to settle before recording. Screen recordings need the full backend running locally.
